🎶 Spotify Most-Streamed Songs 2024: Statistical Analysis

Statistical study of the relationship between playlist count and total streams in Spotify’s 2024 most-streamed songs dataset. The analysis includes data cleaning, descriptive statistics (mean, median, percentiles, standard deviation), correlation assessment, simple linear regression, goodness-of-fit evaluation (R²), and inspection of outliers and their impact on model stability and predictive accuracy.

🎼 Spotify Most-Streamed Songs 2024: Statistical Modeling

A continuation of the previous Spotify analysis, this project explores the distributional characteristics of song popularity using statistical modeling. The focus is on estimating parameters for normal and gamma distributions using the method of moments, evaluating model fit through percentile comparisons, and assessing the reliability of estimates via confidence intervals. The project also includes a hypothesis test to determine whether songs that appear in more playlists tend to receive significantly more streams.
